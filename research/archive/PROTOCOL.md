# Frozen historical replay design

Use the first 20 minutes after 00:00 UTC on the first day of every month in 2024 and 2025. These dates have public Deribit top-five snapshots from Tardis. Use all BTC inverse vanilla calls and puts in the captured prefix, with the first minute as warm-up. The first 12 monthly windows are training, the next 12 are the held-out evaluation. No contract selection by future activity or outcome. Record source URLs and canonical slice digests. Never join two contracts or two dates to form a label.

At a valid two-sided book, sample a prediction of the next distinct midquote direction within ten seconds. Use local receipt time; collapse equal receipt timestamps within each contract to the last snapshot before making decisions. Exclude stale exchange snapshots (over two seconds old) and crossed, empty or malformed books. Break a pending trial at invalid observations. Start at most one trial per contract at a time and wait at least a second between decisions. Close the trial at the first observed changed mid, or censor at timeout/end/invalidity. Drop startup observations from scoring. Publish all exclusion counts and coverage. This is an event-time question conditional on an observable move; it differs from the original fixed-horizon live pilot.

Compare a training-majority rule, the fixed signs of level-one and five-level size imbalance, a logistic baseline with the direction of the most recent mid change, relative spread and call/put type, and the same fixed logistic model with top-level and five-level imbalance added. Use logistic regression with standardized training features, C=1 and no tuning. Primary outcome is paired held-out Brier score improvement over the model without imbalance. Also report directional accuracy, confusion counts, log loss, breakdown by session and call/put. Confidence interval resamples whole paired monthly sessions (4,000 draws, seed 7301). Report both pooled and equal-session results. Repeated contracts share BTC exposure. Interpret the interval as descriptive.

For each labeled prediction compare signed mid movement and a hypothetical immediate round trip crossing the observed spread at both ends, in units of entry spread. State the assumption of immediate infinitesimal fills, lack of fees/latency and conditioning on a price change. No trading-return claim. Use a deterministic shuffled-imbalance diagnostic within held-out session and option type. Preserve all results even if baselines win.

Implementation details fixed before held-out scoring: retain only nonnegative receipt lag, exclude new decisions in the final ten seconds, and use log relative spread. For continuity also score the original five-level sign rule separately on its one-second clock: same contract, first observed quote 1–2 seconds after decision, conditional on a nonzero move and nonzero imbalance, same 2024/2025 split and warm-up, excluding invalid-book crossings. Its training-majority baseline is estimated on eligible training moves. This supplementary replication has a different sampling clock from the primary study.
